Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how Sixth Star Studio ("we") handles data in its internal publishing tool (the "App"), which connects to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by the studio itself to manage its own accounts. It is not available to the public.

What data we access

  • Basic profile information of our own accounts (account ID, name, profile picture).
  • Our own videos and their metadata (titles, descriptions, captions, thumbnails, privacy settings).
  • Aggregated statistics for our own content (views, watch time, likes, comments counts).

We do not collect personal data of viewers or followers, and we do not access other users' accounts.

How we use it

Only to upload and schedule our own videos, edit their details, and measure how they perform.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform it belongs to (for example a video upload to YouTube).

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored locally on the studio's own computers and are never published. Statistics are kept only as long as needed for our own reporting.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the settings of the platform (Google, TikTok or Meta). To request deletion of any data we hold, email sixthstar967@gmail.com; we respond within 30 days.

Platform policies

Use of YouTube data is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. TikTok and Meta data is handled according to their developer terms.
